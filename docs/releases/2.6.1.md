# Elsepin 2.6.1

Build 98
<!-- elsepin-build: 98 -->

Plan a route with a different pace for each leg, rehearse stops, or pick a pin and take the joystick. Build 98 brings tighter movement pacing and more control over how a trip flows.

- **Move at the pace you set.** Routes and the joystick account for elapsed time. A delayed location write avoids a burst of catch-up steps. Use your usual speeds for new routes and joystick starts, and keep custom speeds in saved trips.
- **Add a simulated traffic light.** Enable it at selected stops. Green lets you pass through and red adds a short wait. Fixed stop waits remain available.
- **Bring in a GPX route.** Choose a track, route or waypoint in Files and review the import. The selected name and points carry into the planner. You can also use Files or paste JSON to selectively restore a library backup.
- **Keep your edits.** Change a leg’s mode and choose **Keep path** or **New directions**. Adding a stop from Map search to a saved route keeps its existing Library entry, favorite and folder.
- **Read more of the map.** Search along the trip, use longer pin addresses, and hold the active teleport marker to choose its symbol. The cellular stop confirmation gives you a chance to keep the session active.

## Install

Back up your Library, then sign and install **Elsepin-2.6.1-build98.ipa** on iPhone. Start on Wi-Fi with LocalDevVPN connected and a valid pairing. An established session may continue on cellular while the connection stays usable and iOS keeps the app active. Use **Stop spoofing** to request a clear. If the clear fails, reconnect Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN and try again.
